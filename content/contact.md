## Email

The best way to reach us is email:

**[admin@cdfcrew.it](mailto:admin@cdfcrew.it)**

## What to send

A short note is enough. Helpful details include:

- What you are building and who it is for
- Where you are in the journey (idea, MVP, scaling, maintenance)
- Timeline expectations and any hard constraints

We read every message. If it looks like a fit, we will reply with next steps.
