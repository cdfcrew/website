## Who we are

This website is published by **CDF Crew** (“we”, “us”). You can contact us at
**[admin@cdfcrew.it](mailto:admin@cdfcrew.it)**.

## What this site collects

This is a **static website**. We do **not** use analytics cookies, advertising trackers, or embedded
third‑party widgets on this version of the site.

## Email

If you email us, we will use your message and contact details **only to respond** and to operate any
follow‑up conversation you start. We do not sell personal data.

## Hosting and logs

Your browser will communicate with our hosting provider when you load pages and assets. That provider
may keep **standard server logs** (such as IP address, user agent, and request metadata) for security
and reliability. The exact retention depends on the environment where the site is hosted (for example,
GitHub Pages for previews or a self‑hosted static server for production).

## Your rights (EEA / UK)

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, or restrict processing of
your personal data, and the right to object or lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority. To
exercise a request, email **admin@cdfcrew.it**.

## Changes

We may update this page from time to time. When we do, we will adjust the “Last updated” date above.
